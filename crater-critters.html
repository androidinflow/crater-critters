How to play

←→ / AD walk
↑↓ / WS aim
Enter jump · EnterEnter backflip
Hold Space to charge, release to fire
1–5 Pinecone Bomb fuse
Tab / right-click: weapon picker
Click map: target for Bee, Pelican, Anvil, Thundercloud, Teleport, Girder
Acorn Rifle: laser sight, one big precise shot
Fish Slap: stand next to a foe and whack them into the sea
Skunk Bomb: poison cloud that follows the wind
Mole Drill: dig a tunnel along your aim · Pogo Stick: giant safe jump
Hedge Bowling rolls far · Twig Boomerang comes back (catch it!) · Acorn Mortar: 3-shell burst
Sunbeam burns through terrain · Gravity Acorn pulls everyone in · Chili Breath & Thunder Stomp: close range
Static Jar chains between critters · Freeze Ray costs a turn · Flood raises the water · Critter Cannon fires you
Poison (gas, nettle arrows, the virus) costs 5 HP at the start of each of your turns · a Sentry Turret shoots whoever walks into its sights · Fizz Brick and Mega Banana detonate when you press fire again
Enter jumps the way you are holding · Enter twice or Backspace backflips · hold ←→ in the air to bend a jump
Star Shower: click for a meteor storm · Switcheroo: click a critter to swap places
Girder: ↑↓ rotates the plank
Rope: ←→ swing, ↑↓ climb, Space let go
Jetpack: ↑ thrust, ←→ steer, Space stop
Robot: Space again to detonate
Drag mouse to pan · scroll to zoom
Esc/P pause · H help · M mute · F fullscreen · N minimap
Click the minimap to look around the map
Online: T or the Chat button to talk to your friend
Touch: D-pad walks & aims, hold FIRE to charge, tap the map to target, drag to pan, pinch to zoom
After firing you get 3 seconds to run away!
Water and the map edges are instant doom.
